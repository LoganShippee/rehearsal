Rehearsal for Mac (v1.3.1)

Works on Apple Silicon (M1–M4) and Intel Macs. Includes MuseScore conversion, Google Drive browser, and live window mirroring.

Download Rehearsal.zip

First-Launch Guide (Takes 20 Seconds, One Time Only)

  1. Double-click Rehearsal.zip in your Downloads folder, then drag Rehearsal into Applications. Important: open it from Applications, not Downloads—macOS locks apps left in Downloads, which can block the microphone and automatic updates.
  2. Double-click Rehearsal in Applications. Because the app isn't notarized with a paid Apple Developer ID yet, macOS will say it cannot verify the developer—click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. You only ever need to do this once.
Automatic signed updates: Rehearsal checks for updates automatically, verifies every update with a cryptographic signature, and never touches your classes or files.

Rehearsal for Windows 10 / 11 (v1.3.1)

Installs strictly for your teacher user account (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Rehearsal)—ideal for locked-down school PCs.

Download Installer (.exe) Portable .zip

First-Launch Guide (One Time Only)

  1. Run Rehearsal-Setup.exe (no school IT admin password needed).
  2. If Windows Defender SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC" (because the app isn't EV code-signed yet), click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Rehearsal adds a Start Menu shortcut and stores your classes and 30-day automatic backups safely in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Rehearsal\.
Built-in WebView2 & Google Drive: Works right on Windows 10 and 11, including MuseScore .mscz conversion, full-browser windows, and automatic signed updates.
