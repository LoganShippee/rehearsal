Download Try it

Download Rehearsal

Free for the classroom. No account, no login to teach, and it works offline. Updates are automatic in the apps.

Mac

The Mac app, as a zip file.

Download Rehearsal.zip

  1. Open the zip, then drag Rehearsal into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. macOS may say it cannot check the developer. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and allow Rehearsal. Open it again.

Windows

An installer, or a portable zip.

Download Rehearsal-Setup.exe

Portable version: Rehearsal-Windows.zip

  1. Run Rehearsal-Setup.exe.
  2. Windows SmartScreen may show a warning, because the installer is not signed. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Finish the installer and open Rehearsal.

In your browser

Nothing to install. Works in any modern browser, and offline after the first visit.

Try it in your browser

Or go straight to app.rehearsalmusic.com.

Chromebook and iPad

There is no store app. Open Rehearsal in the browser and add it to your home screen.

Good to know

  • The classroom tools work offline: display, tuner, metronome, sight reading, score practice and grading.
  • Online Homework, YouTube, Google Drive and update checks need internet.
  • Your files and classes stay on your device.
  • The apps update themselves. See what’s new.
