Supported devices

Rehearsal runs on Mac, Windows, Chromebook, iPad and any modern browser. Pick the one that matches the computer on your projector.

Go to Download

Mac

The Mac app, Rehearsal.zip. Unzip it, drag it to Applications and open it. It is the only version that can open MuseScore files (.mscz), if MuseScore is installed.

Windows

The installer, Rehearsal-Setup.exe, or a portable zip you can run without installing. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen shows a warning. Choose More info, then Run anyway. It can open MuseScore files if MuseScore is installed.

Chromebook

Use Open web version in Chrome (it is the full app) and add it to your shelf. There is no app in the Chromebook app store. Nothing to install, so it is easy for a school to allow.

iPad

Use Open web version in Safari (it is the full app) and tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. There is no App Store app. Tap mode works well on an iPad for rhythm grading.

Any browser

The web version works in a modern browser on any computer. It can open MusicXML scores (.mxl, .musicxml, .xml), but not MuseScore files. Open web version, or try the demos.

What works offline

The classroom app is free and needs no account to teach in the Mac and Windows apps.

Works without internet

  • The classroom screen and bell schedule
  • Tuner and drone, and the metronome
  • Sight reading and printable worksheets
  • Score Practice and grading

In the apps, your files and classes stay on the device. The web version asks teachers to sign in and backs up settings and classes to their account.

Needs internet

  • Online Homework
  • YouTube
  • Google Drive
  • Checking for updates

On Online Homework, if the Wi-Fi drops, a student recording is saved on the device and uploads when the connection returns.

Microphone

You only need a microphone to grade singing. Tap mode needs none: students tap with the space bar or a tap pad on the screen.
