What “free in class” means

Do I need an account to use the classroom screen?

Not in the Mac and Windows apps: there is no sign-up or login to teach, and your classes and files stay on your device. The web version asks teachers to sign in so settings and classes are backed up to their account. The web version is still free.

Is the classroom app free for good?

It is free for classroom use. The tuner, metronome, solfege board, sight reading, score practice and grading are all part of it.

Does it need the internet?

Not for the classroom app. It works offline. Online Homework, YouTube, Google Drive and update checks need a connection.

Do my students pay anything?

Students sign in to Online Homework with a class code and their own code. Pricing is for teachers, not students.

Will I be charged without warning?

No. Billing is not switched on. You’ll get notice before anything is charged.
