Frequently asked questions

Short answers for teachers. Can’t find yours? Ask us.

Do I need an account?

Not in the Mac and Windows apps. The classroom screen and tools are free and need no account or login there. The web version asks teachers to sign in with Google or an email code, so settings and classes are backed up to their account. Online Homework uses the same sign-in.

How does the web version save my data?

It is stored in this browser and backed up to your account once you sign in. Library files (scores, PDFs) stay in the browser, so use Export backup now and then. If you are offline, it keeps working and backs up when you reconnect. The Mac and Windows apps keep dated backups on the computer.

What devices work?

Rehearsal runs as a Mac app, a Windows installer, and in a web browser. On a Chromebook or iPad, use Open web version: it is the full app. See Supported devices for details.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The classroom display, tuner, metronome, sight reading, score practice and grading all work without internet. You need internet for Online Homework, YouTube, Google Drive and update checks.

What do students need for homework?

A device with a browser and internet, and a microphone for singing or playing. Students don’t need an email address: a class code plus a personal 6-character student code is enough. Sight reading can also be tapped with the space bar or a tap pad.

How do students sign in?

With the class code plus their personal 6-character student code, or with Google where their school allows it. No student email is needed for codes. Sessions on shared Chromebooks expire after 24 hours.

Where are recordings stored, and for how long?

Recordings made for Online Homework are uploaded to our server, and the audio there is deleted automatically after 90 days. Scores are kept. You can delete audio, a single student, or a whole class at any time. On the student’s device, the newest 5 practice takes per assignment are kept. If Wi-Fi drops, a recording is saved on the device and uploads when the connection returns.

Google sign-in says “Access blocked”. What do I do?

Your school’s Google settings only allow approved apps. Teachers can use the 6-digit email code instead, and students can use their student codes. To allow Google sign-in for everyone, ask your school’s IT to trust Rehearsal. Steps for IT

What does it cost?

The classroom screen and tools are free for classroom use. Online Homework has a 30-day free trial with no card. After the trial it switches to read-only (view grades, export) until a plan is active. Billing is not switched on yet: it’s free during the launch. Planned price is $5 per month or $49 per year per teacher. You’ll get notice before anything is charged. See Pricing.

Can my district review it?

Yes. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking scripts, and no sale of data. The classroom app keeps files and classes on your device. The Schools & Privacy page lists what Online Homework stores and which outside services are used. School purchase orders are supported but set up by hand: request one on that page.

How is grading decided, and can I change it?

Rhythm and pitch are scored and shown separately. You set the leeway with two strictness sliders. Rhythm runs from about ±250 ms (very forgiving) through ±100 ms (standard) to ±40 ms (very strict). Pitch runs from about ±100 cents through ±60 to ±25. Tempo does not change the leeway. A student who is steadily late isn’t marked wrong note by note, and you can listen back with the recording lined up with the beat. You can override any score.

Which score files work?

MusicXML files (.mxl, .musicxml, .xml) work in any browser. MuseScore files (.mscz) work only in the Mac and Windows apps, and only with MuseScore installed. PDFs are for viewing and annotating only.

Does the tuner transpose?

Yes. The tuner and drone work for Concert, B-flat, E-flat and F instruments.

Can I use it on a Chromebook or iPad?

Yes, in the browser. There is no store app, so open Rehearsal in the browser and add it to your home screen. The web version works offline once loaded. See Supported devices.
