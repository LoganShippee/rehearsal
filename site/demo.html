What to try in 60 seconds:

Beyond the browser

What the Mac & Windows Desktop Apps Add

The browser demo covers nearly all of Rehearsal's rehearsal tools. Download the desktop app when you want these extra classroom superpowers:

MuseScore (.mscz) Import

Open native MuseScore files directly in Score Practice—Rehearsal converts them automatically using your installed MuseScore 4.

Google Drive & Full Browser

Browse your school Google Drive inside Rehearsal without OAuth admin blocks, and open any website in a full-browser window.

On Mac, mirror another app's window (like MuseScore) right onto your stage with click-through control, plus automatic 30-day disk backups.
