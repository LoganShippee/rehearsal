How it works

Create a class inside Rehearsal, print cut-out 6-character student codes, and assign exercises straight from Sight Reading or Score Practice.

Students open app.rehearsalmusic.com/hw on any Chromebook, iPad, or phone, record or tap their take, and see instant note feedback.
