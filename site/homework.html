Your class, in one panel

Create a class, add students, print their sign-in codes and assign with one button.

  • Add students by pasting a list, or let them join with the class code
  • Print each student’s sign-in code, or let them sign in with Google
  • Assign with one button (Assign) from Sight Reading or Score Practice
  • See rhythm and pitch scores separately in the gradebook

  • Adjust the leeway for each assignment, and override a score when you know better

Signing in as a teacher

  1. With Google. A Google sign-in page opens in a new tab. Finish there, come back, and your workspace loads by itself.
  2. Or with an email code. We email you a 6-digit code. Use the same email each time and it’s the same account.
  3. If Google says “access blocked”, your school’s Google settings are blocking it. Use the email code, or ask your school’s IT to allow Rehearsal. Steps for IT
Test every assignment yourself. Every class has a built-in Test Student. Click Open as student and try each assignment exactly as your students see it: the sign-in, the practice, the grading and the score. It’s not counted in your grades or your student total, and its work is cleared after 7 days.

What students see

Students sign in with the class code plus their personal 6-character code, or with Google where their school allows it. No student email is needed for codes.

Sample class. Click the picture to open the live student demo: sample data only, nothing is saved, and nothing you sing or play is uploaded.

  1. Sign in. Enter the class code and your student code, or use Google.
  2. Pick an assignment. Open the one your teacher gave you.
  3. Record. Practice as often as you like. If Wi-Fi drops, the recording is saved on the device and uploads when it reconnects.
  4. See your score. Rhythm and pitch are shown separately.

The newest 5 practice takes for each assignment are kept on the student’s device. Sessions on shared Chromebooks end after 24 hours.
