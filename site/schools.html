School-network engineering

Why Rehearsal works reliably behind strict school firewalls

Single-Domain Access

  • Zero third-party CDNs or trackers: No Google Fonts, no external analytics scripts, and no ad networks—nothing for content filters to flag.
  • Edge-proxied downloads: Mac and Windows installers download straight from rehearsalmusic.com/dl/... even if github.com is blocked on your campus.
  • Web App & Student Portal on the same domain: Teachers launch rehearsalmusic.com/app/ and students open rehearsalmusic.com/hw without needing github.io.

Zero-Friction Deployment

  • Windows needs no admin password: Installs into the teacher's user profile (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Rehearsal).
  • Day-1 zero-OAuth sign-in: Teachers use 6-digit email codes; students use 6-character personal codes with zero student email addresses.
  • No Google Drive / Classroom API scopes: Desktop Drive browsing signs in like Safari/Chrome—never blocked by "unverified app" prompts.
