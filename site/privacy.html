Rehearsal Privacy & Student Data Policy

Written in plain language for music educators, families, school administrators, and district Data Protection Agreements (FERPA / COPPA / SOPIPA / NY Ed Law 2-d).

Summary: Rehearsal's local classroom app stays 100% free, offline-first, and collects zero cloud data. Only teachers who opt into the optional Online Homework add-on send class assignments and student recordings to Rehearsal's homework service, and all student audio is automatically purged after 90 days.

1. Local Classroom App vs. Optional Online Homework

  • Local Rehearsal App (Mac, Windows, Web): Runs completely locally on the teacher's device. Classes, schedules, rosters, MusicXML scores, PDFs, and classroom recordings stay in the teacher's local app folder or browser storage. Zero student telemetry, zero crash-beacon tracking, zero ads.
  • Optional Online Homework Add-On: Used only when a teacher signs in to create an Online Homework class, publish assignments, and review student homework takes in a Gradebook. Students access assignments via a web link — no ads, no social features, and no cross-site tracking.

3. 90-Day Audio Auto-Deletion & Early Deletion

  • Automatic 90-Day Expiration: Every student submission audio file in Cloudflare R2 (audio/ prefix) has a strict 90-day lifetime enforced in two places: a Cloudflare R2 Object Lifecycle Rule and a daily 07:00 UTC scheduled cleanup job.
  • Grades Preserved: After 90 days (or early deletion), the audio recording and its storage key are permanently removed while the numeric gradebook score and teacher comments remain for the school term.
  • Teacher Delete Audio Now: Inside any submission review window, teachers can click Delete Audio Now to erase the student's recording immediately while keeping the score.

4. FERPA, COPPA, SOPIPA & NY Ed Law 2-d Commitments

  • School Official Role (FERPA 34 CFR § 99.31(a)(1)): Rehearsal acts solely under the direction and control of the subscribing teacher or school district as an outsourced educational service provider. The school retains full ownership and control of all student education records.
  • COPPA Direct Authorization: For students under 13, schools act as the consenting educational agent exclusively for school-directed music instruction. Personal 6-character codes require zero student emails, usernames, or Google accounts.
  • No Secondary Commercial Use (SOPIPA / State Laws): Student data is never used for targeted advertising, behavioral profiling, or product marketing.
  • Right to Access & Deletion (Always Available, Even in Read-Only Mode): Teachers can export the Gradebook CSV, and a student's complete JSON record is available on request, or teachers can permanently delete a submission, a student, or an entire class (cascading through D1 and R2) at any time — even if a trial or subscription has ended.

5. Security Safeguards

  • Transport & Storage Encryption: All traffic uses HTTPS/TLS 1.2+; Cloudflare D1 and R2 encrypt stored data at rest.
  • Cryptographic Access Controls: Personal student codes use 100,000-iteration PBKDF2-SHA256 with unique 16-byte salts. Session tokens are high-entropy bearer secrets stored only as SHA-256 HMAC digests.
  • Zero Public Roster Enumeration: The Student Portal never displays a public dropdown list of students. Students must provide both a Class Join Code and either their Personal 6-Character Code or verified Google account.
  • Brute-Force Lockouts: 5 consecutive wrong codes trigger a 15-minute cooldown; 10 wrong codes lock code sign-in until the teacher issues a fresh code.
  • Shared-Device Protections: Student sessions auto-expire after 24 hours on shared classroom Chromebooks; teachers have a one-click shared-computer session mode that clears on browser close.
  • Server-Side Score Verification: Auto-graded percentage math is recomputed server-side from note results; True Sight-Read assignments lock and strip excerpts until the server-timed attempt begins.

7. Breach Notification & Privacy Contact

In the event of a confirmed security incident involving unauthorized disclosure of student education records, Rehearsal will notify affected teachers and designated district contacts without unreasonable delay and within 72 hours of confirmation, detailing the scope, mitigation steps, and remediation timeline.

For SDPC National Data Privacy Agreement (NDPA) requests, District DPAs, or student data inquiries, contact privacy@rehearsalmusic.com.
