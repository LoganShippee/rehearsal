Report a bug from inside the app

The quickest way is the bug report option in the app itself. It is opt-in: you see the screenshot first and choose whether to send it. Rehearsal has no automatic crash reporting.

Privacy questions

For anything about student data, deleting a class or a student, or a district review, write to privacy@rehearsalmusic.com. See also the privacy note.

School purchase orders

Purchase orders are set up by hand. Choose "School purchase order" in the form, or see the Schools page.
