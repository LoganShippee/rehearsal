How Recording, Study Timers & Sync Work for Students

Practice Mode (Sight Reading & Score Practice)Open Rehearsal

Students see the sheet music immediately, can press Play to hear their part or solfege/piano reference, practice as many times as needed, and choose Microphone (sing/play) or Tap Rhythm when ready to submit.

True Sight-Read Mode (Assessment)Timed Excerpt Reveal

The music stays hidden behind a study cover until the student clicks Reveal & Start Timer. A visible countdown (15s, 30s, 45s, or 60s set by you) lets them silently study solfege and rhythm, then count-in and recording start automatically at 0:00.

Free 30-Day Trial, Simple Pricing & School Privacy

How does the 30-day free trial work? Just enter your school email inside Rehearsal (Setup → Online Homework) to receive a 6-digit code. Your full 30-day trial starts automatically with up to 200 active students — no credit card required. After 30 days, your data stays visible in read-only mode unless you subscribe ($5/mo or $49/yr) or use a School Purchase Order.
Does Rehearsal's main classroom app stay free? Yes — 100% free forever. Bell schedules, timers, Score Practice, Sight Reading, Piano, Tuner, Metronome, and Recorder on your computer never require an account or subscription.
What if I switch between my school computer and home web browser? Sign in on any device with your school email and the 6-digit code sent to your inbox. All your Online Homework classes, student codes, assignments, recordings, and grades carry over automatically. Each device stays signed in for 30 days (unless you tick "Shared computer"), and you can use Sign Out All Devices anytime in Account & Plan.
Is this safe for school Chromebooks and student privacy laws (FERPA / COPPA)? Students never enter an email address or create a third-party account — only your Class Code + a Student Code. Student audio recordings automatically delete after 90 days (or immediately when you click Delete Audio Now), and school Google admin whitelisting is not needed.
