Rehearsal Online Homework — Student Data Privacy Summary (SDPC / NDPA Brief)

1-Page Administrator & Parent Overview · FERPA / COPPA / SOPIPA / NY Ed Law 2-d Ready
Local Classroom App: 100% Free & Offline
Online Homework: Optional Teacher Add-On

Service Scope & Purpose

Product: Rehearsal Online Homework (sight-reading, music score practice, and vocal/instrumental recording assignments for K–12 music classrooms).

Legal Role: Operates as a designated School Official under FERPA (34 CFR § 99.31(a)(1)) strictly under the control of the teacher/district. Never collects data in the free local app.

No-Email Classroom Mode

  • Zero Student Emails Required: Teachers can generate random 6-character personal codes (e.g. H7P-9KM) stored only as salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes.
  • No Public Rosters: Student Portal never exposes student lists or name dropdowns.
  • Optional Google SSO: Uses openid email profile scopes only — never accesses Drive, Classroom, or Gmail.
Data Element What Is Stored Retention & Deletion
Student Identity Display name (or alias/initials), optional voice part, salted code hash (or optional Google sub + email). Deleted immediately on teacher Delete Student / Delete Class (even in Read-Only Mode).
Grades & Feedback Attempt scores, rhythm/pitch percentages, per-note timing/pitch breakdown, teacher comments & overrides. Exportable anytime as Gradebook CSV or FERPA Student Data JSON; cascades on deletion.
Audio Recordings Student performance audio (WebM / M4A / WAV / Ogg) encrypted at rest in Cloudflare R2. Auto-deleted after 90 days (R2 lifecycle + daily cron) or immediately via Delete Audio Now.
Never Collected Zero behavioral ads, zero third-party ad/analytics trackers, zero sale/licensing of data, zero biometric voiceprints or AI training on student audio, zero student social messaging.

Security Architecture

  • Encryption: HTTPS/TLS 1.2+ in transit; encrypted Cloudflare D1 & R2 storage at rest.
  • Auth & Lockouts: 100k-round PBKDF2-SHA256 codes, HMAC-hashed session tokens, 24h Chromebook session expiry, 5-fail 15m cooldown / 10-fail lock.
  • Anti-Tamper: Server-side score recomputation; True Sight-Read excerpts withheld until timed start.

Sub-Processors & Incident Response

  • Cloudflare (Workers/D1/R2): Core API, database, 90-day audio storage.
  • Resend & Stripe: Teacher login emails & teacher billing only (zero student data).
  • Google LLC: Optional Sign-In identity check only.
  • Breach Notice: Affected schools notified within 72 hours of confirmation.
